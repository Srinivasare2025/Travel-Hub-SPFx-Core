## Cormorant Garamond (font)

Used as the display typeface for the two largest heading tiers (page and
section titles — see `src/common/styles/_typography.scss`), self-hosted as a
base64-inlined `@font-face` in `src/common/styles/_fonts.scss`. The source
`.woff2` is kept at `src/common/styles/fonts/cormorant-garamond-latin.woff2`.
